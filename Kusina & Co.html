More Than Just Food. It's a Taste of Home.

In every Filipino home, the table is where life happens. Birthdays, Sunday lunches, and long evenings with cousins and neighbors all start with a pot simmering on the stove.

Kusina & Co. grew from those tables. We cook authentic family recipes with fresh ingredients and patient technique, the way our lolas did, and serve them in a way that fits modern life.

Every plate is meant to feel like coming home.
